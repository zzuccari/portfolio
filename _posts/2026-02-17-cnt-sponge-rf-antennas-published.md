Our paper "[Interconnection morphology effects on the radio frequency response of carbon nanotube sponges](https://www.beilstein-journals.org/bjnano/articles/17/23)" has been published in the *Beilstein Journal of Nanotechnology*. The work, with [Prof. Manuela Scarselli](https://didatticaweb.uniroma2.it/docenti/curriculum_vitae/5054-Manuela-Angela-Scarselli)'s group at the University of Rome Tor Vergata, compares the RF antenna performance of carbon nanotube sponges (CNSs) against MWCNT films, showing an enhanced response gain and a further improvement after ethanol treatment. See the [Publications](/portfolio/publications/) page for more details.
